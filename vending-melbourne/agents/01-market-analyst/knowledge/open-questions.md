## B. Hand-offs to other agents

**→ 00 Lead Strategist**
- The market is mature, slow-growing (~3–4% CAGR) and fragmented. Advantage comes from **site selection, format (smart fridge/micro-market) and niche positioning**, not market growth.
- The top segments are industrial/logistics shift sites (S1), mid-size workplace smart fridges (S2) and PBSA/apartments (S3). Avoid public transport, universities and public hospitals at the start.

**→ 02 Competitor Analyst**
- Profile: Benleigh Vending, Morsl, Provender (franchise), Agora Markets, and the Coca-Cola Europacific Partners and Asahi/Schweppes direct placement programs (including Coke&Go smart coolers).
- Find out which operators hold the University of Melbourne, Monash, RMIT, Monash Health and Alfred contracts.
- AVBA and IVMOA membership.

**→ 03 Location Scout**
- Priority zones: West (Truganina, Laverton N, Derrimut, Sunshine), South-east (Dandenong South, Clayton) and North (Campbellfield, Tullamarine).
- PBSA and 300+-unit towers in the CBD, Southbank and Docklands.
- CBD office vacancy is 19% (Jan 2026). Deprioritise small CBD offices.

**→ 04 Product & Pricing**
- Build two planograms (shelf layouts):
  - (a) industrial: energy drinks, full-sugar drinks, hearty snacks;
  - (b) Healthy Choices-compliant: ≥50% GREEN, ≤20% RED, no sugary drinks.
- Use the FoodChecker tool.
- Check the CDS Vic 10c deposit effect on drink pricing.
- Ken Research's average ticket is ≈A$6.30, which seems high for classic vending. Verify.

**→ 05 Equipment & Tech**
- Cashless readers are mandatory (RBA: cash is ~15% of payments). Keep cash acceptance at blue-collar sites.
- Evaluate smart fridges (weight/RFID/vision) against spiral machines.
- Telemetry for route optimisation.

**→ 06 Supply & Operations**
- Explore joining the AVBA buying group. Its ACCC collective-bargaining protection runs to 23 Jun 2027, so check whether it will be renewed.
- Fresh-food supply for smart fridges.

**→ 07 Financial Modeller**
- Public benchmarks:
  - A$650–750 gross per machine per month on average (low-confidence seller data).
  - A real Sydney listing: A$61k/yr from 7 machines (≈A$725/machine/month).
  - Margin about 20–40%.
- Segment ESTIMATE: A$800–2,500/month at good industrial sites.
- Average ticket: A$4–5 for classic machines, A$8–12 for smart fridges (ESTIMATE).

**→ 08 Legal & Compliance**
- The Healthy Choices policy directive (Jan 2025) applies to all vending in Victorian public health services.
- Card surcharging rules.
- Owners' corporation approvals for apartment placements.
- CDS Vic obligations.
- Food safety for fresh-food smart fridges (council food-premises registration class).

**→ 09 Devil's Advocate**
- Points to attack:
  - Market-size figures are unreliable (8× spread).
  - Revenue-per-machine data comes from sellers with an interest in high numbers.
  - The "passive income" hype suggests oversupply.
  - Hybrid work is eroding office demand.
  - The cash share is rising slightly, which is a contrarian signal.
