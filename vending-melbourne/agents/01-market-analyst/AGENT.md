# Market Analyst

**ID:** 01 · **Folder:** `agents/01-market-analyst` · **Agent definition:** `.claude/agents/market-analyst.md`

## Mission
Size and characterise the Melbourne / Victorian / Australian vending market and identify the most attractive customer segments.

## Key research questions
- Australian vending industry size, growth, and trends (IBISWorld-type figures, cashless share, smart fridges, micro-markets, healthy vending).
- Melbourne demographics relevant to vending: workforce in industrial zones, students, hospital staff, apartment density, shift workers.
- Consumer behaviour: spend per transaction, cashless adoption, healthy-choice demand.
- Which segments are under-served or growing?
