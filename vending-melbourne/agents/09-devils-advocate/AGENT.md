## Mission
Stress-test every option by finding failure modes, hidden costs and over-optimistic assumptions.

## Key research questions
- Why do small vending businesses fail in Australia? Common scams (location-placement 'turnkey' scams, overpriced machine packages).
- Risks: vandalism, theft, card fee creep, host cancellations, competitor undercutting, inflation, cost of living.
- Hidden costs and time drains beginners underestimate.
- Kill criteria: signals that an option should be abandoned.
