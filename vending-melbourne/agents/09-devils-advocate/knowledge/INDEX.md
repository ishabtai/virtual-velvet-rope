## Top findings

1. **Location is the asset, and the machine is a commodity.** Every documented vending scam, including the ACCC's action against Michigan Group over orange-juice machines it said would go into major supermarkets, sold placements that did not exist (https://www.accc.gov.au/node/14733).
2. **Melbourne "placement included" packages often guarantee a site for only 180 days.** The payback period is about 2.5 to 3.5 years, so the guarantee runs out long before the money comes back. One listing claims 11 machines can be run in "1 hour per week" for $100k + GST.
3. **Honest single-machine economics are thin (ESTIMATE).** At the Australian rule-of-thumb level of $160/week gross, a machine nets about $1.5k/yr before depreciation and labour, and about $770 after depreciation. That works out to roughly $12/h for the owner. At $100/week the machine loses money.
4. **Sites can disappear overnight.** A Sydney operator paid $20k for a site that closed before it earned $16k. A forum buyer had 8 machines removed 30 days after purchase because there was no written contract.
5. **From 1 Oct 2026 card surcharges are banned** on eftpos, Mastercard and Visa (https://rba.gov.au/media-releases/2026/mr-26-10.html). Vending payment fees reported at about 17c per transaction plus $15/month per reader can no longer be passed on as a surcharge, so they must be built into prices.
6. **Open formats lose stock.** Shrink in micro-markets typically runs 4–6% and can reach 10–12% without deterrents (US trade data).
7. **Scale is needed.** Australian operators say real money needs "at least 50 machines". The median beginner with 1–5 machines is running a low-paid side job with capital at risk.
8. **Australia lost $2.18bn to scams in 2025, with investment scams the top category at $837.7m.** No vending-specific Scamwatch statistic exists, so this risk is under-measured rather than absent.
9. **The highest-risk options for a zero-base beginner** are a franchise or package (F), a micro-market (C) and niche novelty machines (D). The cheapest option to test is a small number of classic or cashless machines (A) on month-to-month agreements with pre-set relocation triggers.
