# Kill Criteria — When to Abandon an Option

Owner: 09 Devil's Advocate · Researched: 4 Nov 2026 · Greater Melbourne, AUD

Kill criteria are pre-committed tripwires. Write them down *before* spending money, so sunk-cost bias can't argue later. All thresholds are **ESTIMATE / judgement** unless sourced; 07-financial-modeller should recalibrate them once the base model exists.

---

## 1. Pre-purchase kill criteria (do not proceed if ANY is true)

| # | Kill signal | Applies to | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Seller/locator won't disclose the venue and let you contact it before payment | A, D, F | Core pattern of ACCC Michigan Group and US placement frauds (https://www.accc.gov.au/node/14733; https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/seven-individuals-charged-manhattan-federal-court-connection-multimillion-dollar) |
| P2 | Location guarantee shorter than modelled payback (e.g. 180 days vs 2.5–3.5 yrs) | F, A | Guarantee expires long before capital is returned (payback ~2.5 yrs per RealCommercial "sad reality"; ~3.4 yrs in hidden-costs.md ESTIMATE) |
| P3 | ≥3 points on the red-flag checklist | All | scams-and-red-flags.md §5 |
| P4 | Route seller can't produce ≥12 months machine-level telemetry + bank deposits that reconcile | E | Unverifiable revenue |
| P5 | <70% of a route's revenue sits under written, assignable agreements | E | Contracts are the asset; buyer reports of locations pulled within 30 days (https://vendiscuss.net/topic/14920-what-questions-should-i-ask-re-purchasing-this-full-line-route/) |
| P6 | Price per machine > 2× independent replacement cost and no verified site earnings to justify the premium | E, F | Goodwill unsupported |
| P7 | Modelled break-even requires >$200/week gross per machine at a site with no trading history | A, B, D | Above typical AU benchmark (~$650–750/month gross ≈ $150–175/week, RealCommercial) |
| P8 | Modelled owner hourly rate at base case < national minimum wage (≈$25/h) after vehicle & depreciation | All | Business is a low-paid job with capital at risk |
| P9 | Founder cannot fund 6 months of downside (site loss, move, storage, repairs) without debt stress | All | Liquidity, not profit, kills micro-businesses |
| P10 | Franchise/licence: no disclosure document, or franchisor won't give contacts of ≥5 current *and* former franchisees | F | Franchising Code expectation — hand-off 08 to confirm applicability |

## 2. Early-operation kill/relocate criteria (first 90 days per site)

| # | Signal | Action |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Weeks 1–8 average gross < $100/week (inc GST) | **Relocate** the machine; don't wait for "it'll pick up" |
| E2 | Weeks 9–12 average below modelled break-even weekly gross | Relocate or renegotiate commission |
| E3 | Shrink > 5% of sales for 2 consecutive months (B, C) | Lock down format or exit site |
| E4 | Second break-in/vandalism within 6 months at one site | Exit site |
| E5 | Payment costs > 6% of gross sales | Renegotiate provider or reprice |
| E6 | Host fails to pay/allow access/power, or asks for commission > modelled max | Exit site |
| E7 | Servicing time > 1.5 h/machine/week sustained after month 2 | Re-cluster route or drop farthest site |

## 3. Option-level kill criteria (review at 6 and 12 months)

| Option | Kill if… (at 12 months) |
|---|---|
| A Classic | <50% of machines above break-even after one round of relocation; or portfolio net (after vehicle, depreciation) < $1,000/machine/yr |
| B Smart fridge | Average site gross < ~$300/week inc GST (ESTIMATE given higher capex) or shrink + expiry > 8% |
| C Micro-market | Can't sign a site with ≥100 on-site staff within 6 months of trying (ESTIMATE; hand-off 03 to validate headcount threshold) |
| D Niche | Month 4–6 sales < 50% of month 1–2 sales (novelty decay) |
| E Route | >20% of acquired revenue lost within 6 months of settlement; or actual net < 70% of seller's represented net |
| F Franchise/package | Fees + mandated-supplier mark-ups > 25% of gross; or franchisor-supplied sites lost at expiry of guarantee |

## 4. Whole-venture kill criteria (founder level)

Stop expanding and consider exit if, at the 12-month review:

1. Portfolio net profit after all costs (incl. vehicle, depreciation) ÷ owner hours < 60% of the minimum wage, **and** no clear path (clustering, better sites) to lift it within 6 months.
2. Cumulative cash invested minus cash returned exceeds the founder's pre-set maximum loss (founder must set this number on day 0 — ESTIMATE suggestion: no more than the amount they could lose without changing their lifestyle).
3. More than one third of sites have been lost or relocated in the year (site churn too high to build equity).
4. The founder no longer wants to do the physical work and the business is too small to hire (a casual restocker costs ~$30+/h loaded — ESTIMATE).

## 5. "Green light" counter-criteria (the other side of the coin)

Devil's advocacy is not "never do it". Signals that justify continuing/expanding:

- ≥60% of machines exceed $250/week gross inc GST after 3 months.
- Sites are within a 10–15 km cluster, servicing ≤45 min/machine/week.
- At least one host has referred you to another site.
- Written agreements ≥12 months with ≥30-day termination notice cover most revenue.
- Shrink <3% and payment costs <5%.
