# Maintenance and Security

*Agent 06 · Researched 4 Nov 2026 · AUD*

> **Jargon:** *Coin mech* = coin acceptor/changer. *Bill validator* = note acceptor. *Cashless reader* = card/phone tap device (e.g. Nayax). *First-line maintenance* = simple fixes the operator does on site; *second-line* = technician repairs.

## 1. Routine maintenance schedule (ESTIMATE, based on common operator practice)

| Every visit | Monthly | Quarterly | Annually |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wipe glass, keypad, delivery bin | Clean coin mech and note path | Clean condenser coil (drink/fridge machines) — dust kills compressors | Electrical test & tag (if site requires) |
| Check every spiral vends (test vend 1–2 items) | Check door seals on refrigerated units | Check firmware/telemetry updates | Full service by technician for refrigerated units |
| Check temperature display (cold units) | Check cashless reader signal/receipts | Inspect power lead, plug, surge protection | Review machine placement/profit |
| Remove expired/damaged stock | Review fault log & refund log | | |

- **Test and tag:** many Victorian workplaces require portable electrical equipment testing under their WHS/AS/NZS 3760 regime; site hosts may demand it. ESTIMATE — Agent 08 to confirm whether vending operators are legally required or only contractually.

## 2. Common faults and first-line fixes

| Symptom | Likely cause | First-line fix |
|---|---|---|
| Product hangs in spiral | Wrong spiral pitch for product, item loaded backwards | Re-load; adjust spiral; use drop sensor machines |
| Coins rejected/jammed | Dirty coin mech, foreign object | Clean; clear jam; replace mech (spare on van) |
| Notes rejected | Dirty sensors, worn belts | Clean with validator cleaning card |
| Card reader offline | Mobile signal, SIM, power | Reboot; check signal; antenna relocation; contact provider |
| Drinks warm | Blocked condenser, door seal, failing compressor | Clean coil; check seal; call refrigeration tech |
| Display/keypad dead | Power, fuse, board | Check power; reset; technician |

- **Spares kit (ESTIMATE ~$500–1,500):** spare coin mech, spirals in common pitches, fuses, keypad, cleaning supplies, test product, cable ties, torch, multimeter.
- **Technicians:** second-line repairs from machine suppliers or refrigeration technicians. Ask the machine seller (Agent 05) for Melbourne service coverage and response times before buying — this is a critical supplier selection criterion. Typical refrigeration call-out in Melbourne ~$120–200 + labour (ESTIMATE, not sourced).

## 4. Security measures by site risk

| Site risk | Example sites | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Inside staffed offices, secure staff rooms, hospitals behind access control | Standard locks; cashless + coin |
| Medium | Gyms, apartment lobbies, schools, sports clubs | Cashless-preferred, small coin float, CCTV coverage from host, tamper alerts via telemetry |
| High | 24/7 public-access (laundromats, car washes, train stations, outdoor) | Cashless-only, anchored/bolted machine, reinforced door/lock (T-handle high-security lock), vandal-resistant glass or glass-free front, telemetry door-open alerts, host CCTV |

- **Lock management:** keyed-alike locks per route; record key holders; change cores if a key is lost. ESTIMATE best practice.
- **Insurance:** public & product liability is commonly required by site hosts (Melbourne full-service operators advertise it as included). https://www.vendingmachinesaustralia.com.au/melbourne-vending-machines — also consider contents/theft cover for machines off-premises. Agent 08 to price.
- **Location contract:** clarify who pays for damage/vandalism and whether the host provides CCTV/after-hours security.

## 5. Cash handling

- Collect cash on every visit; never leave large sums overnight in machines.
- Count at the base, not in the vehicle; bag and bank weekly; reconcile cash vs telemetry sales (a gap = theft, jam or miscount).
- Coin is costly to bank (bank coin-deposit fees exist; amounts not verified — search budget exhausted). Many operators recycle coins as change floats. ESTIMATE.
- Cashless share: payment-provider fees (e.g. US-listed Nayax 5.95% on ≤$5 transactions) are often cheaper than the labour/risk of handling cash at small volumes, though not always. Agent 05/07 to compare with AUD quotes.

## 6. Vandalism and response protocol

1. Telemetry alert or host call → attend within 24 h (sooner if glass broken or food exposed).
2. Photograph damage; report to Victoria Police (online report for property damage) for insurance; get report number.
3. Remove stock/cash; secure or disable the machine; tag "out of order".
4. Claim insurance / invoice host if contract allows.
5. If repeat (≥2 incidents in 6 months), relocate the machine. ESTIMATE threshold.

## 7. Workplace health & safety for the operator

- Manual handling: drink slabs ~9.5 kg each; use trolleys and lift with legs; avoid carrying >15–20 kg per trip. ESTIMATE.
- Moving machines (250–400 kg typical) requires professional movers/pallet jacks — never DIY. ESTIMATE; Agent 05 to supply weights.
- Driving fatigue on long route days; WorkSafe Victoria obligations apply once you employ anyone. Agent 08.
