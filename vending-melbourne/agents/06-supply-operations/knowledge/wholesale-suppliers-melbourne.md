# Wholesale Suppliers — Greater Melbourne

*Agent 06 Supply Chain & Operations · Researched 4 Nov 2026 · All prices AUD*

> **Jargon:** *Cash & carry* = a trade-only warehouse where you pay on the spot and take the stock yourself. *Broadliner* = a large foodservice distributor (PFD, Bidfood) that delivers thousands of lines to cafes and institutions. *COGS* = cost of goods sold, the landed cost of each item in the machine. *Ex-GST* = before the 10% Goods and Services Tax.

## 1. The short answer for a zero-machine beginner

| Stage | Machines | Best sourcing mix | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start | 1–5 | Costco business membership + supermarket half-price specials (Coles/Woolworths) + Campbells (once ABN held) | No minimum orders, buy only what you need, cash/card. |
| Grow | 5–20 | Campbells / Metcash cash & carry as core, Costco for drinks and multipacks, start talking to beverage reps | Better per-unit prices on confectionery; supplier relationships begin. |
| Scale | 20+ | Direct accounts with beverage bottlers (Coca-Cola Europacific Partners, Asahi/Schweppes, Frucor Suntory), confectionery distributors, a broadliner for chilled/fresh lines | Delivered stock saves driver hours; volume rebates and free/loan fridges possible. |

ESTIMATE: the machine thresholds above are judgement based on minimum-order behaviour described below, not a published rule.

- **Lesson:** shelf price swings ~30–60% between full price and specials. A small operator who buys **only on half-price weeks** and holds 2–4 weeks of stock can approach cash & carry pricing. ESTIMATE based on the swing above.
- Supermarkets frequently apply per-customer purchase limits on specials; not a scalable channel past ~10 machines. ESTIMATE.

## 9. GST notes

- Most snacks/confectionery/soft drinks are **taxable** (GST applies); plain water and some basic foods may be GST-free. Once registered for GST (mandatory at $75k turnover), the operator claims back GST on taxable stock purchases and must remit GST on taxable sales. Hand-off to Agent 07/08 for exact classification.

## 10. Practical sourcing checklist

1. Get an ABN (needed for Campbells and most trade accounts).
2. Join Costco business; register at Campbells; open a Coles/Woolworths rewards account to track specials.
3. Build a price book (spreadsheet) of the top 30 SKUs across 3 suppliers; refresh monthly.
4. At ~10 machines, call CCEP and Asahi reps and ask about vending pricing, delivery minimums and branded machine programs.
5. For chilled lines, test PFD/Bidfood/Complete Food Services minimums before committing to a smart fridge.
