# Scaling Operations — When to Hire, Outsource, or Buy a Route

*Agent 06 · Researched 4 Nov 2026 · AUD*

## 1. Operating stages (ESTIMATE framework)

| Stage | Machines | Founder hours/week (ESTIMATE) | Ops model |
|---|---|---|---|
| Side-hustle | 1–5 | 3–10 | Own car, home storage, Costco/supermarket specials, cash + cashless |
| Part-time operator | 5–15 | 10–20 | Small van or wagon, Campbells account, telemetry on all machines, pre-kitting |
| Full-time owner-operator | 15–35 | 30–45 | Van, storage unit, beverage direct deliveries, routes by day |
| Small fleet | 35–80 | Owner manages; 1–2 drivers | Warehouse/storage, VMS software, technician on call |
| Multi-route | 80+ | Management role | Multiple drivers, possibly a fresh-food kitchen partner |

Hour benchmarks: 1–5 machines ≈ 3–10 h/week; 10–15 locations ≈ 15–20 h/week; a driver services 10–14 machines/day manually or 20–25 with pre-kitting (US sources). https://vmfsusa.com/blogs/business/when-to-bring-on-help-a-second-person-for-restocking-the-complete-vending-route-scale-blueprint ; https://www.upperinc.com/success-stories/vendmax-services/

## 2. Labour options and costs

### a) Employee (casual restocker/driver)
- **Award uncertainty:** the correct modern award for a vending restocker is not settled here — possibilities include the General Retail Industry Award (MA000004) or the Storage Services and Wholesale Award. Agent 08 must confirm.
- Reference rate (General Retail Award, adult Level 1, from 1 July 2026 after a 4.75% increase): **$27.81/h permanent, $34.76/h casual** (incl. 25% loading); casual Sunday $48.67, public holiday $69.53. Third-party sources; confirm with Fair Work pay guide. https://www.rosterelf.com/downloads/rosterelf-general-retail-award-pay-rates-2026-27.pdf ; https://fairworkmate.com.au/tools/pay-rates/retail-worker-vic
- On-costs: superannuation guarantee (12% from 1 July 2025), WorkCover Victoria premium, payroll admin; plus vehicle allowance if using own car. ESTIMATE: total cost ≈ 1.2–1.3× base hourly.
- **Cost per machine visit (ESTIMATE):** at ~2.5 machines/hour incl. driving, casual at $34.76 + ~15% on-costs ≈ **$16 labour per visit** (vehicle extra).

### b) Contractor paid per machine
- A Melbourne job listing offered **$50 per vending machine serviced** (shopping-centre sites, restock + clean). https://au.indeed.com/Vending-Machine-jobs
- Pros: variable cost, no payroll. Cons: sham-contracting risk if the worker is really an employee (Agent 08); $50/visit is expensive versus employee cost for small machines, but may include travel and vehicle.
- ESTIMATE: per-visit contractor makes sense only for remote/outlier machines or holiday cover.

### c) Outsourced full-service vending company
- Melbourne operators already offer "free" full-service vending to hosts (deliver, install, refill, insure); they monetise product margin. https://www.vendingmachinesaustralia.com.au/melbourne-vending-machines ; https://sites.google.com/view/venharvending/home ; https://australianmanufacturing.com.au/business-directory/allsorts-vending
- For a new owner, **outsourcing servicing of owned machines to an existing operator** is possible in principle (sub-contract), but no public price list was found. ESTIMATE: expect a % of sales (perhaps 20–35%) or per-visit fee. Ask Allsorts/VENHAR-type operators. Also note these firms are competitors for sites (Agent 02).

### d) Family/partner help
- Common in small routes; still subject to WHS and possibly employment law if paid.

## 3. Triggers to hire help (ESTIMATE decision rules)

Hire or contract a restocker when **two or more** apply:
1. Route time exceeds ~20 h/week and the founder's time is better spent selling new locations.
2. More than 2 stock-out complaints/month due to missed visits.
3. Gross profit per week covers the restocker's cost at least **3×** (labour should stay under ~15–20% of sales). Agent 07 to validate.
4. Fresh food or smart fridges require daily visits.
5. Founder holiday/sick cover needed (single point of failure).

## 4. Systems to put in place before scaling

| System | Needed by | Notes |
|---|---|---|

- SEEK shows ~14–24 Melbourne vending listings depending on filter. https://www.seekbusiness.com.au/businesses-for-sale/within-vending/in-melbourne-vic-3000

**Operational due diligence for a route purchase:**
1. Ride along on 2+ full route days; time each stop.
2. Obtain telemetry/sales history per machine (≥12 months), not owner summaries.
3. Inspect site contracts: term, commission %, termination clauses, assignability to a new owner.
4. Machine age, refrigeration condition, cashless readers, spare parts availability.
5. Supplier terms that transfer (beverage accounts, delivery).
6. Check location density — a cheap route spread across Melbourne may cost more in driving than it earns.
7. "Placement included" starter packages: verify placement quality and re-site guarantees; these packages are a known risk area (Agent 09).

## 6. Fresh food / micro-market scaling note

- Fresh requires daily visits, ≤5 °C cold chain, Class 3 registration, and a food supplier (commercial kitchen, PFD/Bidfood, or local makers). At scale, operators often partner with a commissary kitchen. Shrinkage risk is the main scaling barrier (see inventory-management.md).
