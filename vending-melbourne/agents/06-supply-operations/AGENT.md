# Supply Chain & Operations

**ID:** 06 · **Folder:** `agents/06-supply-operations` · **Agent definition:** `.claude/agents/supply-operations.md`

## Mission
Design how stock is sourced and machines are serviced efficiently in Melbourne.

## Key research questions
- Wholesale sources in Melbourne (Costco, cash & carry, Metcash/Campbells, direct distributors, specialist vending suppliers) and account requirements.
- Restock route design, visit frequency, time per machine, vehicle and storage needs.
- Inventory management, expiry/spoilage, theft and vandalism handling, refunds.
- When to hire help; outsourced servicing options.
