## Mission
Coordinate the team, resolve conflicts between findings, and synthesise the final set of business-model options with clear trade-offs and a go/no-go framework.

## Key research questions
- What vending business-model archetypes exist (classic route, smart fridge/micro-market, niche/specialty, buy existing route, franchise, location-only/placement)?
- How do successful small Australian operators typically start and scale (1 → 5 → 20 machines)?
- What decision criteria should separate the options (capital, time, risk, scalability, defensibility)?
- What does a sensible 90-day test plan look like for each option?
