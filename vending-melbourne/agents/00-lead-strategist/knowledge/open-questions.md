## B. Unknowns that could change the recommendation (ranked)
1. **Realistic gross sales per machine in Melbourne** by site type (warehouse, gym, office, uni, aged care). Only one quoted figure ($650–750 a month) plus a mature route (~$1,530 a month) were found.
2. **Founder's actual budget, hours and risk tolerance.** These need to be elicited before Phase 4.
3. **Availability and real pricing of existing Melbourne routes** today. The 62-machine route is under offer; the $500k run listing may be stale.
4. **Smart-fridge economics in Australia:** shrinkage, waste and minimum staff numbers. Only US/UK benchmarks were found.
5. **Saturation:** how many operators already cover Melbourne industrial parks? Are good sites still winnable?
6. **Actual franchise fees and terms** (Provender, Ausbox, Just Now). Only resale listing prices were seen; disclosure documents are needed.
7. **Placement-package success rate:** what share of "secured sites" survive 12 months?

## D. Questions for the founder (Phase 2 intake)
1. How much cash could you invest, and how much could you lose without hardship?
2. How many hours a week, and when (weekdays, weekends)?
3. Do you own a car, ute or van? Do you have storage (garage)?
4. Any network in workplaces, gyms, universities or industrial sites?
5. Willing to handle fresh food?
6. Goal: side income, replace salary, or build to sell?
7. Preferred area of Melbourne (to cluster the route near home)?
