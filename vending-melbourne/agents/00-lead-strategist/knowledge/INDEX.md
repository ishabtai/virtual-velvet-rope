## Top findings

1. **Location beats machine.** A mature Melbourne route (62 machines, trading since 2008) claims ~$95k a month, about $1,530 per machine, at a 65–69% gross margin. That's roughly twice the ~$650–750 a month quoted for a typical Australian machine. (case-studies.md §1, §3)
2. **A small fleet is side income.** The Sydney teen's best machine netted ~$12k a year. His own rule of thumb is 50+ machines for a living. One person can realistically run ~40–50 clustered machines (Andres Vending, 46). (case-studies.md)
3. **Entry cost is low for DIY.** Used machines cost $2–4k and new combo machines $6.9–8.8k. A 1–3 machine test costs about $5–12k. (archetypes A)
4. **Placement packages (~$20–36k for 2–5 machines) charge a premium for "secured sites".** They follow a pattern regulators have pursued before. Verify sites and get a re-site guarantee. (archetypes F)
5. **Existing routes sell for roughly 2–3.5x SDE.** Melbourne and Brisbane examples: $100k for 11 machines, $500k for a run, $600k WIWO for 60 machines. Verify through telemetry and BAS. (archetypes D)
6. **Micro-markets and smart fridges need 80–150+ staff per site**, and in Australia they are run by B2B providers (Morsl, Provender at $70–140k franchise resales). The fit is the warehouse belts in the west and south-east. (archetypes B)
7. **Health-service sites must meet Victorian Healthy Choices:** ≥50% GREEN, ≤20% RED, no RED drinks in combo machines. (archetypes B)
8. **Niche concepts are fragile.** Pizza Gio closed, and the Southern Cross pizza machine was a pop-up. Gacha is booming (Capsule Station) but faced child-related concerns in Jun 2026. (archetypes C)
9. **Draft scoring:** A Classic DIY 3.50 leads, then D Buy route and G Asset-light (3.20 each). The suggested default path is **A → D (learn, then buy)**. (decision-framework.md)
10. **Evidence gap:** no verified Australian P&L was found, and page fetching was blocked. Key numbers need re-verification. (open-questions.md)
