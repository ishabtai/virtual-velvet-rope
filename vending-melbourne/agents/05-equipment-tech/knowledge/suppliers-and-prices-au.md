## 3. Used-machine buyer checklist
1. **MDB-compliant controller?** (essential for card readers)
2. **Refrigeration**: holds ≤4–5 °C under load (test for 24 h; food machines need temperature compliance — Agent 08).
3. **3G legacy**: Australia's 3G networks shut in 2024; old telemetry modems and card readers died — any machine with 3G hardware needs a 4G reader/telemeter **and** a new SIM [26][27].
4. Parts availability in AU for that brand (Sanden, Crane National, Fastcorp, TCN, Bianchi, Necta are common) [19].
5. Power: standard 10 A 240 V plug; check current draw.
6. Physical: fits through site doors (most units ~1.8 m H × 0.9–1.2 m W × 0.85 m D, 250–350 kg — ESTIMATE); transport needs a tail-lift truck and a pallet jack/stair-climber (ESTIMATE $200–$500 per move in Melbourne metro).
7. Locks and keys present; programming manual available.
8. Buy from a dealer offering 3-month warranty in preference to private sale.
