# Finance, Leasing & Tax Treatment of Equipment

_Research date: 2026-11-04 · AUD · Not financial or tax advice — Agent 07 (finance) and Agent 08 (legal) to validate; founder should see an accountant._

## 1. The five ways to get a machine

| Option | Upfront | Ongoing | Who owns it | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Buy outright – used** | $1,250–$5,500 [1][2] | Repairs | You | Cheapest; fast payback; IAWO-eligible | Reliability, 3G/MDB upgrades, no warranty |
| **Buy outright – new** | $5,500–$13,000 + payment [3][4] | Lower repairs | You | Warranty, efficient, modern UI | Capital tied up in unproven sites |
| **Rent from supplier** | Low/zero | e.g. snack $25.87/wk, combo $31.50/wk, beverage $59.17/wk, food $73.38/wk [5][6] | Supplier | Test sites cheaply; swap machines | Term/residual unclear; ~$1,350–$3,800/yr adds up |
| **Equipment loan / chattel mortgage** | 0–20% deposit | Monthly repayments; rates "generally 5–10% p.a." (guide estimate) [7] | You (lender holds security) | Keep cash; GST claimable upfront; interest + depreciation deductible | Credit check; vending is a hard asset class for lenders [8] |
| **Finance lease / operating lease** | Low | Fixed monthly; residual (balloon) | Lender | Off-balance-sheet-ish, predictable | Total cost higher; locked in |
| **Franchise / turnkey** | e.g. AIPVend from $29,970 + GST per machine [9] | Royalties/fees | You (usually) | Sites + training included | Expensive per machine — compare to DIY carefully (Agent 02/07) |
| **Payment-device rental** | $0 | Nayax VPOS Media 4 < $1/day [10] | Nayax | Removes ~$500–$800 reader cost | Ongoing fee |

## 2. Lending environment (late 2026)
- Brokers advertise **no-deposit / 100% finance** for vending equipment through partner lenders (marketing claim) [11].
- Lenders typically want a personal **credit score of ~640+** for start-up asset finance [11].
- Supplier commentary: tighter lending has made vending machines harder to finance as an asset class [8].
- Start-ups with no ABN trading history (<2 years) usually face **higher rates (ESTIMATE 9–16% p.a.)** and may need a personal guarantee; "low-doc" loans exist at higher rates.
- **ESTIMATE example:** $10,000 machine, 3-year chattel mortgage at 12% p.a., no balloon → ~**$332/month** (~$77/week). Compare to supplier rental of $31.50–$59.17/week.

## 3. Tax (ATO) — what matters for equipment
- **Instant Asset Write-Off (IAWO):** Small businesses (aggregated turnover < $10m) using simplified depreciation can immediately deduct assets costing **under $20,000 each** (threshold applies per asset). The 2026-27 Budget announced making $20,000 **permanent from 1 July 2026** [12][13]. Sources conflict on whether it is fully legislated as at Nov 2026 [13][14] — **Agent 07/08 must check the ATO page before relying on it.**
  - Most single vending machines (<$20k) qualify → full deduction in year of first use/installation.
- **GST:** If registered (mandatory at $75k turnover; voluntary below), the 10% GST on machine purchases is claimable as an input tax credit on the BAS. Under a chattel mortgage, GST is claimable upfront; under a lease, GST is spread across payments (ESTIMATE — standard treatment, confirm with accountant).
- Rental/lease payments are generally deductible operating expenses.

## 4. Decision guide by founder budget
| Founder budget | Suggested equipment finance |
|---|---|
| < $10k cash | Buy 1–2 used MDB combos outright; Nayax rental reader; no debt |
| $10–$30k cash | Mix: 2 new combos bought + 1–2 rented machines to trial new site types |
| Wants to scale fast, $30k+ | Chattel mortgage on new machines/smart fridges; keep cash buffer for stock and slow sites |
| Wants least effort | Franchise/turnkey — but compare per-machine cost (AIPVend ~$30k vs DIY ~$10k) |

**Principle:** don't buy a machine before you have a signed placement agreement — a machine without a site earns nothing (Agent 03). Rental or refurbished stock lowers the cost of a site that fails.

## 5. Resale / exit value (ESTIMATE)
- Used machines resell at 30–50% of new [2]; well-maintained MDB combos with 4G readers resell fastest.
- Smart fridges: resale market thin and tied to the vendor's software licence — check licence transferability.
- A route (machines + placement contracts) sells for a multiple of earnings (see Agent 02/07 for business-for-sale comps).
