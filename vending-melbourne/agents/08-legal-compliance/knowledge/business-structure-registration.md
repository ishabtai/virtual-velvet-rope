# Business Structure, ABN, GST and Business Name

> General information only, not legal, tax or financial advice. Get advice from a registered tax agent and/or a solicitor before choosing a structure.
> Research date: 4 Nov 2026. Fees are indexed every 1 July, so check them again after 1 July 2027.

## 1. Why structure matters for a vending business

A vending business has three features that affect the choice of structure:

1. **Liability exposure.** Machines sit in other people's premises, sell food and use mains power. A tipped machine, an electrical fault or a contaminated product can lead to a claim. A company structure limits personal exposure. Insurance is still the main protection (see `insurance.md`).
2. **Capital-heavy start.** Machines cost thousands each (see agent 05). Depreciation and instant asset write-off rules affect cash flow.
3. **Thin margins and a long path to profit.** Early years may run at a loss. A sole trader can usually offset a business loss against other income, subject to the non-commercial loss rules (background knowledge, verify with ATO). A company keeps losses inside the company.

## 2. Structure options compared

| | Sole trader | Pty Ltd company | Discretionary (family) trust + corporate trustee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Set-up cost (govt fees) | ABN free; business name $47 for 1 yr / $108 for 3 yrs (from 1 Jul 2026) | ASIC registration **$636** (from 1 Jul 2026) + business name if trading under another name | Trust deed (~$300–$1,000, ESTIMATE) + company for trustee ($636) |
| Ongoing govt fees | Business name renewal | ASIC annual review **$342/yr** (from 1 Jul 2026) | Annual review for trustee company + separate trust tax return |
| Tax rate | Personal marginal rates (tax-free threshold applies to residents) | **25%** if a "base rate entity" (turnover < $50m and passive income ≤ 80%), else 30% | Income distributed to beneficiaries at their rates |
| Personal liability | **Unlimited.** Personal assets are exposed | Limited, but directors can be personally liable (insolvent trading, director guarantees on leases or supplier credit, director penalty notices for unpaid PAYG/super) | Limited if the trustee is a company |
| Accounting cost | Lowest. ~$500–$1,500/yr, ESTIMATE | ~$2,000–$4,000/yr, ESTIMATE | ~$3,000–$5,000+/yr, ESTIMATE |
| Best for | Testing with 1–3 machines on a small budget | Growing a route, taking on a partner or investor, or signing larger host contracts | Founders with family members on lower tax rates; more complex |

### Practical recommendation pattern (ESTIMATE, not advice)
- **Pilot phase (0–5 machines, under about $30k invested):** a sole trader is cheapest and simplest. Hold **$20m public and products liability** insurance from day 1.
- **Scale phase (a route, a franchise, an investor, or any personal guarantee):** move to a Pty Ltd. Restructuring later can trigger CGT/stamp-duty issues and means re-signing host agreements, so if the founder already expects to scale, starting as a company saves rework.
- Host agreements and supplier accounts should be in the name of the entity that will own the machines.

## 3. ABN (Australian Business Number)
- Apply through the Australian Business Register / Business Registration Service (business.gov.au). It is **free** (background knowledge, verify).
- You need an ABN to register a business name, register for GST, and avoid suppliers or hosts withholding tax from payments (no-ABN withholding at the top marginal rate, background knowledge).
- You must actually be carrying on an enterprise. A genuine intention backed by steps taken (for example a machine quote or a site negotiation) is normally enough. Verify on the ABR.

## 4. Business name (ASIC)
- If you trade under any name other than your own personal name (sole trader) or the exact company name, you must register it with ASIC.
- Fees from 1 Jul 2026: **$47 for 1 year, $108 for 3 years** (up from $45 / $104 in 2025–26). Source: https://www.vjmglobal.com/feeds/blog/asic-company-registration-fee-2024 ; 2025–26 figures confirmed at https://help.nowinfinity.com.au/hc/en-au/articles/12968633575183-ASIC-Fee-Indexation-effective-1-July-2025
- The fee is charged **per name**.
- A business name does **not** give trade-mark protection. If the brand matters (for example a branded smart-fridge network), consider an IP Australia trade mark. Check the current fee with IP Australia; it is not researched here.
- Check that the name and the `.com.au` domain are available before printing machine wraps.

## 5. GST

### Registration threshold
- Registration is **compulsory once GST turnover reaches $75,000** in any 12-month period, either current or projected. GST turnover is gross income, excluding GST, not profit.
- You must register within **21 days** of becoming aware you have crossed, or will cross, the threshold. The ATO can backdate registration.
- Voluntary registration below $75k is allowed.
- Sources (secondary, consistent with ATO rules): https://itp.com.au/the-sole-trader-gst-registration-australia-2025-guide/ ; https://sparkreceipt.com/en-au/blog/gst-registration-threshold-australia/ . Verify on ato.gov.au.

### Should a new vending operator register voluntarily?
- **Pros:** you can claim back the GST (input tax credits) on machines (about 1/11th of a $5k–$15k machine, so roughly $450–$1,350 each), stock, fuel and repairs. Many sites, such as corporates and councils, expect suppliers to be GST registered.
- **Cons:** you must remit 1/11th of taxable sales. Vending prices are usually fixed round numbers ($3.00, $4.50), so GST comes out of margin. You also lodge a BAS (Business Activity Statement) quarterly.
- **Rule of thumb (ESTIMATE):** with only a few machines and mostly taxable products, registering early is usually worth it in the set-up year because of the large GST credits on equipment. Model it with agent 07.

## 6. Other tax registrations and obligations
- **PAYG withholding:** needed only when you hire employees.
- **Superannuation guarantee:** 12% of ordinary time earnings for employees from 1 Jul 2025 (background knowledge, verify). Contractors may also count as employees for super purposes.
- **WorkCover (WorkSafe Victoria):** compulsory once you pay wages above the threshold (about $7,500 a year in recent years, background knowledge, verify). This does not apply to a solo sole trader with no workers.
- **Victorian payroll tax:** threshold of about $900k+ in wages. Not relevant at start-up.
- **Income tax / instant asset write-off:** the $20,000 small-business instant asset write-off ran to 30 Jun 2026 per background knowledge. **Status for 2026–27 is unverified.** Agent 07 should check before modelling. Otherwise machines are depreciated over their effective life.
- **Cash handling:** the ATO targets cash-heavy businesses (the "shadow economy"). Keep telemetry and cash-count records that reconcile to bank deposits. Cashless-first machines make this easier.

## 7. Step-by-step set-up checklist
1. Decide the structure (accountant consult, about $200–$400, ESTIMATE).
2. Register a company with ASIC (if a company) → apply for an ABN.
3. Register the business name (if needed).
4. Decide on voluntary GST registration.
5. Open a business bank account and merchant/cashless account (agent 05).
6. Take out insurance **before** the first machine is placed.
7. Notify or register with the council via FoodTrader (see `food-act-victoria.md`).
8. Sign a host agreement for each site (see `host-agreement-template.md`).
9. Set up record keeping: per-machine sales, stock, GST code, cash reconciliation, test-and-tag log, temperature log (smart fridges).
