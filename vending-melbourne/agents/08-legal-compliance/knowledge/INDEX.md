## Top findings
1. **Classic snack/drink vending (Class 4) carries very little food-law burden.** You notify your principal council once via FoodTrader. It is free, valid statewide, never renewed, and needs no Statement of Trade. (FoodTrader; Banyule and Buloke ABLIS listings.)
2. **Adding pre-packaged sandwiches or pies makes the business Class 3:** annual council registration (examples $194 at Glen Eira and $307 at Greater Dandenong for 2025–26) plus Statements of Trade. Making your own food or dispensing unpackaged high-risk food makes it **Class 2**, which needs a Food Safety Program and a Food Safety Supervisor. The highest-risk item sets the class.
3. **Every machine must display the operator's name and business address** (Food Standards Code 1.2.1 vending rule). Combine this with an ACL refund contact, because "no refunds" signs are unlawful.
4. **Structure:** a sole trader is cheapest for a pilot. A Pty Ltd (ASIC $636 + $342/yr from 1 Jul 2026; 25% base-rate tax) suits scaling, route purchases or investors. GST is compulsory at $75k turnover. Voluntary registration is often worth it in year 1 to claim GST back on machines. Confectionery, chips and soft drinks are taxable; plain water and plain milk are generally GST-free.
5. **Insurance:** $20m public and products liability is the de-facto entry ticket for hosts and councils (Merri-bek $10m, Cardinia $20m minimums). Budget about $700–$1,500/yr for 1–3 machines (ESTIMATE; small-business PL+products typically ~$605/yr per Upcover). Standard packs often don't cover machines in third-party premises.
6. **Card surcharges:** from 1 Oct 2026, Visa, Mastercard and eftpos can ban surcharging. Build card costs into the price; don't add a card fee.
7. **Importing machines or drinks yourself creates regulatory obligations.** Machines must carry the RCM and may need EESS registration by an Australian Responsible Supplier (the founder if importing). Directly importing drinks makes you a **CDS Vic first supplier**, with a contract, reporting and payments. Buying from local distributors avoids both.
8. **Host agreements:** get a written licence agreement for every site, covering term, commission base (net of GST and refunds), power, access, proportionate indemnity, an unprofitability exit, and **assignability** (key to route resale value). Unfair contract terms laws (penalties from Nov 2023) apply to standard-form contracts with small-business hosts. Commission benchmark is 0–20% (US data; no Australian survey found).
9. **Public land (footpaths, reserves) needs a council local-law permit.** Example: Merri-bek $380 application plus $10m PL. Councils may simply refuse, so get approval before buying outdoor equipment.
10. **Exclude tobacco, vapes and alcohol.** Tobacco vending now needs a Victorian retail licence (enforced from 1 Feb 2026) and makes the host responsible for under-18 sales. Avoid facial recognition in smart fridges, given privacy enforcement against Kmart and Bunnings.
