## Precinct notes

### Dandenong South (A)
- The Dandenong NEIC employs **more than 66,300 people** (https://www.planning.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0031/628366/plan-melbourne-map-dandenong-national-employment-innovation-cluster.pdf).
- Dandenong has "almost 1,400 manufacturing businesses and more than 21,000 workers" (https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/2022-08/220817%20-%20Manufacturing%20The%20Future%20In%20Dandenong.pdf, Aug 2022). Greater Dandenong has the highest number of manufacturing businesses of any Victorian municipality (council claim via search, https://www.greaterdandenong.vic.gov.au/).
- Major corporations continue moving in (Savills commentary: https://www.urban.com.au/expert-insights/investing/major-corporations-head-to-dandenong-south-savills).
- Why A: density of shift-working factories within a small drive radius, so one restock run can cover 15–25 sites (ESTIMATE). It also has the South East Melbourne Manufacturers Alliance (SEMMA, https://semma.com.au/) as a networking channel.
- Nearby extensions: Keysborough, Braeside, Carrum Downs, Hallam, Lyndhurst.

### Laverton North / Truganina / Derrimut / Ravenhall (A)
- The Truganina Employment Precinct is about 19 km west of the CBD on the West Gate corridor, in Wyndham, and is part of the "Western Industrial Node" (real-estate listing via search; promotional).
- Recruiter listings show heavy demand for forklift, logistics and production roles in Truganina and Laverton North, with BevChain among the employers (https://randstad.com.au/jobs/q-logistics/victoria/truganina/temporary; https://www.randstad.com.au/jobs/q-driver/victoria/laverton-north).
- Characteristics: very large, modern DCs (20,000–70,000 m²) and many 24/7 operations. Fewer, larger sites than Dandenong. **Gap:** no precinct job count found; check ABS Census place-of-work by SA2.

### Tullamarine / Melbourne Airport (A-)
- APAM has developed the ~410 ha Melbourne Airport Business Park for more than two decades. It includes a 74,200 m² Techtronic Industries warehouse. Only 3.33% of freehold industrial land in the Tullamarine precinct is undeveloped (search snippets citing https://www.realestatesource.com.au/?p=70409 and related property press).
- More than 14,000 people were directly employed in airport-related roles across ~200 businesses (2013 figure, stale).
- Pipeline: MAB's Haystone business park (38 ha, 800+ jobs; https://www.mab.com.au/news/mab-provides-businesses-a-gateway-to-melbourne-with-the-launch-of-its-latest-business-park/) and Elite Park (32 ha, ~2,140 jobs; https://melbourneairport.com.au/corporate/melbourne-airport-unveils-proposed-elite-park-development).
- Note: airport-land sites may sit under the airport lessor's retail controls. Tenant lunchrooms are still the tenant's decision (ESTIMATE; confirm with 08).

### Clayton / Monash NEIC (B+)
- About **75,000 jobs** (https://invest.vic.gov.au/opportunities/precincts/monash-national-employment-and-innovation-cluster). The City of Monash recorded **83,483** jobs in the Monash Precinct in 2021 (https://monash.vic.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/business/documents/city-of-monash-economic-profile-2023-web.pdf). Monash University cites ~95,000 jobs and 13,000 businesses (https://www.monash.edu/news/articles/networking-the-monash-research-and-innovation-ecosystem).
- Mix: Monash University (tendered), CSIRO, Australian Synchrotron, business parks (Monash Business Park / Mulgrave), and manufacturing in Clayton South and Notting Hill.
- Beginner angle: business-park tenants with 50–200 staff and no on-site café, plus purpose-built student accommodation around campus.
- The Suburban Rail Loop East (Clayton/Monash stations) is a construction-workforce opportunity. Construction compounds are temporary but captive (ESTIMATE).

### Port Melbourne / Fishermans Bend (B)
- The 230 ha Fishermans Bend NEIC is targeted to support **40,000 jobs and 20,000 tertiary students by 2050** within 80,000 total Fishermans Bend jobs (https://www.vic.gov.au/fishermans-bend-business-precinct). This is a forecast, not current employment.
- Existing anchors: Boeing, Siemens, port logistics. The University of Melbourne School of Engineering campus is planned on the former GMH site (https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/delivering-new-fishermans-bend-and-jobs-tomorrow).
- Current reality: mixed older industrial sites and depots near the port, plus growing apartment and BTR stock. Moderate potential today with a growth upside.

### Parkville Biomedical Precinct (C for beginners)
- Invest Victoria: 49,000 employed and 7,000+ biomedical, health and medical students per year across the wider precinct (https://invest.vic.gov.au/opportunities/precincts/parkville-precinct). The Victorian Government says up to 73,000 people work in Parkville (https://www.vic.gov.au/parkville-precinct). CSL is moving its global HQ there (https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/major-csl-move-bolsters-world-leading-parkville-precinct).
- Barrier: anchor tenants are public hospitals (Royal Melbourne, Royal Children's, Royal Women's, Peter Mac) under the Healthy Choices directive with tendered retail, plus a University of Melbourne campus.
- Beginner angle: independent research institutes, private specialist clinics and student accommodation in Parkville, Carlton and North Melbourne.

### CBD / Docklands (C)
- Large employers and long hours (operator page: https://www.vendingmachinesaustralia.com.au/docklands-vending-machines). But cafés and convenience stores are saturated, and hybrid work persists (ESTIMATE). Exceptions worth trying: 24/7 car parks, BTR towers, and back-of-house staff areas in hotels and venues.

## Suggested launch geography (ESTIMATE)
- **Option 1 — South-East cluster:** Dandenong South + Keysborough + Braeside + Clayton South. Dense, diverse and manufacturer-heavy, with SEMMA networking.
- **Option 2 — West cluster:** Laverton North + Truganina + Derrimut + Altona North. Fewer, bigger, 24/7 DCs, so higher volume per site with longer drives.
- **Option 3 — North cluster:** Tullamarine + Campbellfield + Somerton.
- Recommendation: pick **one** cluster within about 30 minutes of the founder's home or storage unit. Route density (sites per hour of driving) is a bigger profit lever than any single great site (hand-off to 06/07).
