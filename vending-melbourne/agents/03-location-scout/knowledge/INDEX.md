## Top findings
1. **Industrial and logistics sites are the best beginner target.** They combine 24/7 shifts, captive staff and few food options. The Dandenong NEIC alone employs 66,300+ people, including ~21,000 manufacturing workers across ~1,400 firms.
2. **Pick one cluster** (South-East: Dandenong South/Keysborough/Braeside, or West: Laverton North/Truganina). Route density matters more than any single site.
3. **The Australian host commission benchmark is about 10% rebate, paid half-yearly** (AU business-for-sale listings). Many small sites accept 0% as a "free staff amenity". Tendered sites run 15–30%+ (ESTIMATE). Calculate commission ex GST.
4. **Public hospitals are constrained.** Victoria's Healthy Choices directive removed red (sugary) drinks from vending from 30 Sep 2022, and these sites are tendered. Schools are similarly restricted. Avoid both early on.
5. **Universities, TAFEs, stations and council leisure centres are tender-driven** (e.g. Melbourne Polytechnic's 2025 vending tender). Treat them as a later-stage goal.
6. **24/7 gyms, BTR apartments, laundromats and car washes are cluster fillers.** They have low-to-mid volume and easy approval. Gym incumbents such as Fitness Vending already exist.
7. **"Turnkey" vending packages often have unsecured locations** ("locations being finalised"). One 62-machine Melbourne network implies about $350 per machine per week (unverified).
8. **A new DC fit-out is the best moment to pitch.** Use leasing news and job ads ("night shift", "forklift") to find leads.
9. **Several key sources could not be fetched** (blocked domains, search budget exhausted). See `open-questions.md` for items to verify.
10. **Phase 2:** Healthy Choices is confirmed mandatory for public health services only (including PSRACS), so private hospitals and private aged care are accessible. Use 07's $100/$175/$300 weekly gross for planning. The best archetypes on site winnability are A→D (4.5), D (4) and A (3.5).
