# Location Scout

**ID:** 03 · **Folder:** `agents/03-location-scout` · **Agent definition:** `.claude/agents/location-scout.md`

## Mission
Identify and rank location types and specific Melbourne areas for placements, and learn how to win site hosts.

## Key research questions
- Location types and their economics (gyms, warehouses/industrial estates, hospitals, universities/TAFE, apartments, offices, car washes, laundromats, transport hubs, sports clubs).
- Specific Melbourne suburbs/precincts with high potential (e.g. Dandenong South, Laverton North, Tullamarine, Port Melbourne, Clayton, Parkville medical precinct).
- Typical host commission/rent arrangements in Australia.
- How to pitch and win locations; scoring criteria for a site.
