# Product & Pricing Analyst

**ID:** 04 · **Folder:** `agents/04-product-pricing` · **Agent definition:** `.claude/agents/product-pricing.md`

## Mission
Determine what to sell, at what price, and the margins, by location type.

## Key research questions
- Best-selling vending SKUs in Australia (snacks, drinks, healthy, protein, coffee, niche items).
- Wholesale cost vs typical vend price; gross margin by category.
- Victorian Container Deposit Scheme (CDS Vic) impact on drink pricing.
- Healthy-vending guidelines (e.g. Victorian Healthy Choices for hospitals/health services) and planogram principles.
- Niche product opportunities (PPE, phone chargers, Asian snacks, frozen meals, hot food).
