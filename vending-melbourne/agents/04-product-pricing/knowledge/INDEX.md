## Top findings
1. **Price point:** a 600 ml soft drink in Melbourne vending is ~$4.00–5.00 (modal $4.50); retail anchors are $4.40–4.50 (BWS, Chemmart). Premium only works at captive sites.
2. **Margins (ESTIMATE):** combo snack/drink machine weighted gross margin ~45–58% before commission; ~25–35% after a 15% site commission, card fees and shrink. Water and cans are best; 600 ml Coke worst unless bought direct/on promo. Coffee 62–83% GM; fresh food 35–55% before 5–15% waste.
3. **Buying channel beats pricing:** supermarket shelf price leaves no margin; promo/Costco/direct-bottler buying cuts unit cost 30–60% (e.g. Costco water ≈ $0.30/bottle vs $2.45 shelf).
4. **CDS Vic:** non-alcoholic drink prices rose ~9.1c (soft drinks 9.4c, water 5.1c) in year one; supplier container price rose from 11.18c to 14.7c ex-GST on 1 Feb 2025. Pass it through; never import drinks yourself without CDS registration.
5. **Healthy Choices:** all Victorian public health service vending must have **no RED drinks** (majority GREEN) — 100% compliance reported Feb 2026; guidelines require ≥50% GREEN / ≤20% RED food. Free FoodChecker tool rates SKUs. Compliance skill opens hospital, council, leisure, corporate sites.
6. **GST:** confectionery, chips, soft/sports/flavoured drinks and hot food are taxable; plain still water GST-free (but "consumed on premises" caveat — verify). Model ex-GST revenue.
7. **Melbourne niche edge:** Asian snacks/drinks and cup noodles for campuses and Asian-heavy suburbs; gym protein lines; fresh smart fridges as phase 2; avoid hot food as a first venture.
8. **Seasonality:** chocolate melts in non-refrigerated machines in Melbourne summer; cold drinks peak Dec–Mar, noodles/coffee/chocolate in winter.
9. **Data gap:** no public Australian SKU sales or wholesale price data — first 8–12 weeks of telemetry and real supplier quotes must replace ESTIMATES.
