## 1. Definitions for beginners
- **Smart fridge:** A locked glass-door fridge. Customer taps a card/app to unlock, takes items, door closes; weight sensors, RFID tags or AI cameras work out what was taken and charge the card. Holds fresh food (sandwiches, salads, meals) that spiral-coil machines can't.
- **Micro-market:** Open shelves + one or more fridges + a self-checkout kiosk (scan barcode, pay by card). Like a mini convenience store inside a workplace. Needs a "closed" (staff-only) space because theft is controlled by trust + CCTV.
- **AI cooler:** A smart fridge using cameras/computer vision (e.g. Coke&Go).
- **Unattended retail:** The umbrella term for all of these plus vending.

Smart fridges sit between a snack machine and a full micro-market in cost and product range (general description: https://connectvending.co.uk/blog/what-is-a-commercial-smart-fridge — UK source used only for definitions).

## 3. Who wins where (ESTIMATE)

| Site type | Likely incumbent | Beginner chance |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate HQ, 300+ staff, CBD tower | Compass, Morsl, Nouri, Provender | Low — tender processes, references needed |
| Logistics/warehouse 100–400 staff (Truganina, Dandenong, Laverton) | Machines Vending, Ausbox, SVA, local route operators | Medium — contested but frequent site churn; 24/7 shift work suits fresh-food fridges |
| Office 30–150 staff, suburbs/fringe CBD (Richmond, Cremorne, Collingwood, Box Hill) | Fragmented locals | **Medium–high** — too small for micro-markets, well-suited to one smart fridge |
| Build-to-rent / apartment towers, student accommodation | Provender (apartments); otherwise thin | **Medium–high** — under-served; needs body-corporate / building manager approval |
| Hospitals, universities | Compass, large operators, Coke | Low–medium |
| Gyms, trade schools, childcare staff rooms | Locals (SVA places at kinders/schools) | Medium |

Reasoning: micro-markets generally need a minimum headcount to cover fresh-food spoilage and kiosk cost; one smart fridge can serve smaller sites. Hand-off to 05 (equipment cost) and 07 (break-even headcount).

## 4. Competitive dynamics
1. **Funding has arrived.** Morsl's $5m Series A and Coke's AI-cooler rollout mean well-capitalised players are pushing into smart fridges/micro-markets. Expect higher host expectations (fresh food, app, healthy range) by 2027. `ESTIMATE`.
2. **"Free" is still the host price.** Nouri ("free installation") and Machines Vending ("$0 cost hire") show even expensive smart-fridge/micro-market kit is placed free. A new entrant must fund kit itself.
3. **Fresh food supply is the hidden moat.** Micro-market operators need daily fresh food from a commissary or partner kitchen. A beginner without a fresh supply partner will be limited to ambient/frozen. Hand-off to 06 Supply Chain.
4. **Theft/shrinkage** is the micro-market risk; smart fridges reduce it. Data not found for Australia.

## 5. Gaps observed (detail in `gaps-and-positioning.md`)
- Smart-fridge offer for **20–100 staff** sites is under-marketed. Most micro-market pages target large workplaces.
- **Residential (build-to-rent, student housing)** micro-markets have only one visible Melbourne player (Provender).
- **Night-shift/24-7 hot or fresh meals** for logistics precincts: claimed by Machines Vending/Ausbox, but no evidence of strong fresh-meal offers.
- **Local brand curation** (Melbourne bakeries, coffee roasters, healthy brands) not visibly used as a selling point by any player found.
