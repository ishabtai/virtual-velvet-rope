> This file is **analysis**. Facts reference the other files in this folder (which carry source URLs). Gap conclusions are `ESTIMATE`s based on what competitors visibly advertise; absence of evidence in search is **not** proof a gap exists. Each gap needs field validation (hand-offs listed).

## 1. The core competitive problem
Every Melbourne operator found — from Coke Vending to one-person VENHAR — offers the host the **same deal**: free machine, free install, free restocking, free maintenance, cashless payment (`national-operators.md` §4, `local-operators.md` §2). Commission ranges from "optional"/0% to ~10% of gross (SVA). So a new entrant **cannot win on "free"**. It must win on one or more of:

1. **Segment** — sites incumbents ignore or serve badly.
2. **Product** — range incumbents don't stock (fresh, healthy, local, culturally specific, PPE, etc.).
3. **Service** — measurable reliability and responsiveness.
4. **Commercial terms** — better commission/reporting for the host.
5. **Format** — smart fridge / micro-market where others still run spiral machines.

### 2.4 Commercial-term gaps
- Most small operators pay 0% or "optional" commission; SVA pays 10% six-monthly. A **transparent monthly commission with a live dashboard** is an easy differentiator for sites over a revenue threshold. `ESTIMATE` — hand-off to 07 to set the threshold so commission doesn't kill margin.
- **Staff-subsidy model** (employer tops up prices as a perk, e.g. "free fridge Fridays") — offered by large micro-market players overseas; no Melbourne small-operator evidence. `ESTIMATE`.

## 3. Positioning options (for 00 Lead Strategist)

| Option | Positioning statement | Primary competitors | Capital intensity | Fit for beginner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. "Smart fridge for the 50-person office"** | Fresh, healthy, local food in a single AI/weight-sensing fridge for mid-size workplaces in inner-ring suburbs | Locals with spiral machines; Nouri/Machines Vending at the margin | Medium–High (fridge kit) | Good if fresh-food supplier secured |
| **B. "Night-shift kitchen" for logistics precincts** | 24/7 fresh + hot/frozen meals + drinks for warehouses in the west/south-east | Machines Vending, Ausbox, SVA, 62-machine route | Medium–High | Medium — contested, but high volume per site |
| **C. "Residential micro-market"** for BTR/student towers | Convenience store in the lobby | Provender | High | Lower — longer sales cycle with building owners |
| **D. "Reliable classic route" in one dense precinct** | Classic snack/drink combos, best-in-class service, telemetry, monthly reports; dense cluster (e.g. Moorabbin–Braeside or Dandenong South industrial) | All locals | Low–Medium | **Best for low budget**; low differentiation |
| **E. "Buy and upgrade a route"** | Acquire an established route, retrofit cashless/telemetry, prune weak sites, densify | Sellers in `routes-for-sale.md` | $150k–$500k+ | Good for higher budget; due-diligence-heavy |
| **F. Niche novelty** (cultural snacks, sports nutrition, local-brand showcase) | Distinctive machine in high-footfall venues | Novelty one-offs | Low–Medium | Medium; site-dependent, fad risk |
| **G. Franchise / licence (Provender, Ausbox)** | Buy a system | — | $30k–$40k+ | Easy start; fees and territory limits; buyer's own due diligence |

### Recommended shortlist for Phase 2 (ESTIMATE)
1. **D → A progression:** start with a dense classic route in one industrial/office precinct, add smart fridges at the best-performing sites.
2. **E** for founders with capital and less time.
3. **B** as a targeted niche if 06 confirms a fresh/frozen meal supply partner.

## 4. Defensive considerations
- **Incumbent response:** big operators can match commission at a site they value; defend with relationships and reporting rather than commission wars.
- **Placement-package churn:** each year new package-buyers enter, often with scattered sites; many will want to exit later — **a pipeline of cheap machines/sites** to acquire. `ESTIMATE`.
- **Coke&Go AI coolers** may raise host expectations of format; plan for card/app payment and modern hardware from day one.
- **Tobacco/vape vending** — not an option (Victorian regulation; hand-off 08).
