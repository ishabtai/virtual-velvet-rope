## 1. Jargon used in this file

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Operator** | The business that owns/stocks the machine and keeps most of the revenue. |
| **Site host / location** | The business that lets the machine sit on its premises (office, factory, gym, school). |
| **Commission / rebate** | Share of sales the operator pays the host. Usually a % of **gross** sales (before costs). |
| **Free placement / full-service** | Operator supplies, installs, fills and fixes the machine at no cost to the host. This is the default offer in Melbourne, so a new entrant cannot charge hosts. |
| **Unattended retail** | Industry umbrella for vending machines, smart fridges and micro-markets (no cashier). |
| **Micro-market** | A small self-serve "shop" (open shelves + fridges + self-checkout kiosk), usually inside a workplace. |

## 2. The landscape in one paragraph

Melbourne's vending market has three tiers: (1) **beverage-brand-owned fleets** led by Coca-Cola Europacific Partners' *Coke Vending* arm; (2) **national contract-catering / multi-site operators and franchise systems** (Compass Group Australia, Provender, Ausbox Vending Group, SVA Vending, RP Vending as equipment supplier); (3) a long tail of **small local owner-operators** (see `local-operators.md`). Almost everyone advertises the same thing to hosts: *free machine, free install, free restocking, free maintenance, cashless payment*. Differentiation is weak, which is the key strategic fact for a new entrant.

**Implications for a new entrant**
- Coke Vending targets high-volume sites (airports, transport, large workplaces, hospitals) where cold drinks dominate. A beginner will not win those on price or product breadth. `ESTIMATE` — reasoning: brand owner earns manufacturer margin *and* retail margin, so it can out-commission any independent on drink-only sites.
- Its machines are typically **beverage-only, single-brand** (Coke portfolio). Hosts wanting snacks, fresh food, healthy, local brands or competitor drinks (e.g. Pepsi/Schweppes ranges) are under-served by this tier. `ESTIMATE`.
- AI-cooler rollout raises the bar on "modern-looking" equipment hosts expect.

## 6. Key takeaways
1. **Free full-service placement is table stakes.** No host in Melbourne should pay for a machine; a new entrant competes on service, range and commission.
2. **Coke Vending owns cold-drink-heavy, high-traffic sites.** Avoid head-to-head competition; target sites wanting mixed/healthy/fresh/local ranges.
3. **10% of gross sales** is the most commonly cited Australian host commission (SVA). Generic range 0–30%. `ESTIMATE` that sub-50-staff sites usually take 0–10%.
4. **Several "national" names are really business-opportunity sellers** whose packages create many small new competitors each year (a supply of motivated sellers later — see `routes-for-sale.md`).
5. **Large enterprise sites are locked** behind catering/FM tenders (Compass) — out of reach for a startup.
