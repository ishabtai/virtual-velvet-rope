## Top findings
1. **"Free full-service" is universal.** Every operator, from Coke Vending to one-person locals, offers free machine, install, restocking and maintenance. A new entrant cannot charge hosts and must differentiate on segment, product, service or commission.
2. **Host commission benchmark: ~10% of gross sales** (SVA Vending, paid 6-monthly); small locals often pay 0%/"optional". Generic range 0–30%.
3. **Coke Vending dominates drink-heavy, high-traffic sites** and is rolling out several hundred Coke&Go AI coolers across AU/NZ (from 2025). Large enterprise sites are locked by Compass/Morsl-type tenders. Beginners should avoid both.
4. **Most "vending businesses for sale" are placement packages, not routes**: ~$6k–$11k per machine incl. site (cluster ~$9–10k), no trading history, often scattered sites. A small 2–3-machine route sold at ~1.1× revenue (≈2.3× pre-labour profit, `ESTIMATE`).
5. **Genuine established routes are rare but exist**: 62 machines, ~$1.14m/yr revenue, one owner + student restockers (under offer, price undisclosed); others advertised from $150k, $200k, $500k. `ESTIMATE` fair value 2–3× SDE.
6. **Smart fridges/micro-markets are attracting capital** (Morsl $5m Series A; Nouri, Machines Vending offering free/$0-hire micro-markets) but target large workplaces — **20–100-staff sites are under-served** by fresh-food formats.
7. **Industrial west/north Melbourne is the most contested** local battleground (SVA, VENHAR, Ausbox, Machines Vending, the 62-machine route).
8. **Service proof is a gap**: no Melbourne operator found publishing response-time guarantees, telemetry-based restocking or host sales dashboards.
9. **Franchise/licence entry is cheap but not free**: Provender $30k–$40k+; Ausbox ~$35k–$37k on 5+5-year terms.
10. **Recommended Phase-2 shortlist (`ESTIMATE`)**: dense classic route in one precinct → add smart fridges; buy-and-upgrade a route; night-shift fresh/hot meals for logistics precincts.
