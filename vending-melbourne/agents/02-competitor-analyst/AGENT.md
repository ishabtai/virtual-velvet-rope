# Competitor Analyst

**ID:** 02 · **Folder:** `agents/02-competitor-analyst` · **Agent definition:** `.claude/agents/competitor-analyst.md`

## Mission
Map who already operates vending in Melbourne, what they offer site hosts, and where the gaps are.

## Key research questions
- Major national operators active in Melbourne and their offers (commission, free install, product range).
- Small/local Melbourne operators, smart-fridge and micro-market providers, niche vending players.
- Businesses for sale (existing vending routes) and typical asking multiples.
- Gaps: segments, suburbs, product categories or service levels competitors handle poorly.
