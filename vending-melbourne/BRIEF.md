# Project Brief — Melbourne Vending Business

## Situation
- Founder is starting from **zero**: no machines, no locations, no supplier accounts.
- Geography: **Greater Melbourne, Victoria, Australia**.
- Goal: design a viable business model around vending (broadly defined — classic snack/drink machines, smart fridges, micro-markets, niche/specialty vending, buying an existing route, franchise).
- The founder has **not** fixed budget, time commitment or niche. The team must present **options** across these dimensions rather than assume one.

## Ground rules for every agent
1. Australian / Victorian context first. Prices in **AUD**, include GST treatment where relevant.
2. Cite sources (URL + date accessed) for every factual claim and number. Mark estimates clearly as `ESTIMATE` with reasoning.
3. Date of research: November 2026. Flag anything that may be stale.
4. Write for a beginner operator: explain jargon once.
5. Each agent owns its folder under `agents/` and writes only there.
6. Every knowledge folder has an `INDEX.md` listing its files and the 5–10 most important findings.
7. End each knowledge base with `open-questions.md`: what you could not verify and what other agents should know (hand-offs).
